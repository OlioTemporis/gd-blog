## 3

- **אב** AB (_av_): pater, πατήρ (title for Chokmah's abstract nature)
- **בא** BA, venīre, īre

## 4

- **אבא** ABA (_abba_): dad/daddy (title for Chokmah's manifest nature, as mate to Binah)
- **בב** BB: hollow; a vein
- **גא** GA (_ge'_): superbus

## 5

- **אד** AD (_ed_): mist, vapor
- **גב** GB (_gab_): the back of something or someone

## 6 (sum of 1-3; hence mystic number of Binah)

- **גבא** GBA, cólligō, cóngregō
- **גג** GG, a roof; by extension the top of an altar; Gog, the giant whose partner is Magog
- **דב** DB (_dob_), ursus, ἄρκτος
- **הא** HA, lo, behold; ah!, alas!

## 7 (Venus)

- אבד ABD (_abad_), to perish, vanish, destroy, be lost
- דאב DAB (_daab_), to become faint, languish
- דבא DBA (_dobeh_), riches, power
- אהא AHA, a name of GOD attributed to Venus. Initials of Adonai ha-Aretz.
- או AV, desire; either, or
- גד GD, Gad, a Tribe of Israel (Aries); good fortune
- דג DG, fish

## 8 (2^3; the number of Abra-Melin sub-princes, and of the Servitors of Oriens; Mercury)

- אבה ABH, to will, intend
- אהב AHB (also AVA), desired, beloved
- באה BAH, the entrance, threshold
- דאג DAG, to be anxious, grieve
- דד DD, love; beloved, breast; pleasures of love
- אז AZ, then
- זא ZA, Nqn. Zauir Anpin 478 q.v.

## 9 (since 3^2, Saturn, Binah; Luna, Yesod)

- אוב AVB, ventriloquus: the special 'fire' of black magic, whence Obi, Obeah. Cf. 11 and 207.
- אזא AZA, accendō, incendō, inflammō
- אח ACh, frāter
- בגד BGD (_beged_), vestīmentum
- גאה GAH, as a verb, _gaah_, magnificō; as an adj, _geeh_, superbus; as a noun, _geah_, superbia
- גו GV (_gev_), the middle
- הד HD, Splendor. Cf. 15.

## 10 (sum of 1-4, hence Mystic # of Chesed; Elementorum Sphaera; The # of Abra-Melin Servitors of Amaimon and Ariton)

- אט AT, gentleness, softness; as an adverb, quiet, slow or gentle movement; enchanter, sorceror
- בגה BGH, [Vide K.D. L.C.K. p. 185]
- גבה GBH (_gabah_), 1. to soar 2. be lofty 3. (figuratively) to be haughty
- דאה DAH, flew, soared
- דו DV, two
- הה HH, window; the letter Heh
- זאב ZAB (_zeeb_), lupus
- חב ChB, a hidden place; bosom
